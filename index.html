# Rimind Privacy Policy **Last updated:** [DATE] Rimind ("we," "our," or "the app") is a relationship-maintenance app that helps you stay connected to the people you care about. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how it's protected. ## Information We Collect **Contacts.** With your permission, Rimind accesses your device's contact list so you can choose which people to add to your constellation. We only store the contacts you explicitly add — we do not upload or store your entire address book. **Voice notes.** When you record a voice note about a contact, the audio is uploaded securely, transcribed, and processed to extract relevant details (such as things they care about, how you usually connect, and upcoming events). The extracted text is saved to your account; raw audio may be retained or deleted after processing, depending on your settings. **Account information.** We collect your email address and basic profile information to create and manage your account. **Usage data.** We collect basic app usage information (such as feature interactions and login activity) to improve the product and diagnose issues. ## How We Use Your Information We use the information you provide to: - Build and display your personal "constellation" of relationships - Generate gentle reminders ("nudges") to reach out to people you care about - Improve app features and fix bugs - Communicate with you about your account We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your contacts or voice notes for advertising. ## Third-Party Services Rimind uses the following third-party services to operate: - **Anthropic (Claude API):** Voice note transcripts are sent to Anthropic's API to extract structured details (e.g., topics, reminders, relationship context). Anthropic processes this data on our behalf and does not use it to train their models under our commercial terms. - **Supabase:** We use Supabase to securely store your account data, contacts, voice note transcripts, and app data, with row-level security so only you can access your own data. ## Data Storage and Security Your data is stored using industry-standard security practices, including encryption in transit and access controls that restrict data to your account only. While no system is 100% secure, we take reasonable steps to protect your information. ## Your Choices - You can delete individual contacts, voice notes, or your entire account at any time from within the app. - You can revoke contacts permission at any time through your device settings. - You can request a copy or deletion of your data by contacting us at [SUPPORT EMAIL]. ## Children's Privacy Rimind is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. ## Changes to This Policy We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify you of significant changes via the app or email. ## Contact Us If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at: [SUPPORT EMAIL]
